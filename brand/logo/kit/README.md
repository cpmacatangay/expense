# Salo — Logo Kit

## Direction

**Salo S** — a bold custom S built from the same rounded geometry as a shared table. The identity is
warm, exact, private, and quietly Filipino. No food, receipt, or money clichés.

## Delivery variants

`variants/` contains:

- `salo-symbol-black.svg`, `-white.svg`, `-mono-1f5c45.svg`, `-mono-f7fbf4.svg`
- `salo-symbol-square.svg` — 256 × 256 avatar/social master
- `salo-symbol-favicon.svg` — tight small-size drawing
- `salo-symbol-app-icon.svg` — green tile with cream S
- PNG exports at 32, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px
- Horizontal, stacked, and wordmark black/white/green/cream SVGs
- 1200 px PNG exports of the lockups and wordmark

`web-icons/` contains the favicon, Apple touch icon, 192/512 icons, maskable icon, web manifest,
head snippet, and web SVG variants.

`palette.json` contains the brand colour tokens.

## Android

`android/` contains the production vector layers:

- `ic_launcher_foreground.xml` — cream S on transparent
- `ic_launcher_background.xml` — Salo Green tile
- `ic_launcher_monochrome.xml` — Android 13+ themed icon layer
- `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher.xml`
- `mipmap-anydpi-v26/ic_launcher_round.xml`

These are also installed in the app at:

- `app/src/main/res/drawable/`
- `app/src/main/res/mipmap-anydpi-v26/`

## Guidelines

See `brand-guidelines.md` for colour, clear space, minimum sizes, approved backgrounds, misuse, and
the Android icon specification.

## Notes

- The masters are filled-outline SVG, not live text and not stroke-based.
- The wordmark is custom lettering; do not retype it in a system font.
- The logo is one colour. Terracotta is a supporting accent, not a logo fill.
- Trademark clearance is recommended before commercial use.

## Recommended pairing

- App icon: green tile, cream S, monochrome themed layer.
- In-app header: horizontal lockup, green on cream.
- Recaps and social: stacked lockup or symbol on cream.
- Dark surfaces: cream/white reversed mark.
