# Contract: Shareable Recap

**Module**: `src/features/outings/recap.ts` | **Consumers**: outing detail, share flow

A recap is generated from a finalized outing only. It never includes draft data.

## Required Data

- Outing date, place, total, headcount, and per-head amount.
- Each adult's display name, share amount, and covered student names.
- The payer.
- Simplified settlement suggestions for the outing, or the current group balances when the outing
  is shared from the ledger.
- Currency formatted as Philippine pesos, e.g. `₱1,234.56`.

## Text Format

```text
Salo — {place or "Meal"}, {date}
Total: ₱{total} ({headcount} heads × ₱{perHead})
Paid by: {payer}

{adult1}: ₱{share1} — covered {student names or "none"}
{adult2}: ₱{share2} — covered {student names or "none"}
{adult3}: ₱{share3} — covered {student names or "none"}

Settle up:
{from} → {to}: ₱{amount}
...

Generated by Salo. Receipt photos stay on our devices.
```

Rules:

- Omit the "Settle up" section when there is nothing to settle.
- Use "none" rather than an empty list.
- Use the confirmed total, never a rounded display total, for the per-adult shares.
- Keep the text under 1,200 characters for chat apps.
- Do not include student contact details, notes, or receipt images.

## Image Card

- Size: 1080 × 1350 px (4:5), safe margin 72 px.
- Visual style: receipt-paper card, cream background, ink text, monospaced tabular amounts, one
  accent color, dashed separators.
- Content, top to bottom:
  1. Salo wordmark and outing date.
  2. Place and total, with the total as the largest element.
  3. Per-head line.
  4. Four adult rows: name, covered students, share.
  5. Payer line.
  6. Settlement lines or "All settled."
  7. Small footer: "On-device receipt scan · Private group."
- Use system-safe fonts; do not depend on a network font.
- Render with the Canvas 2D API at 1080 × 1350; no DOM-capture dependency.
- The image must be readable at 360 px wide in a chat preview.

## Share Behavior

- Offer **Copy text** and **Share image** actions.
- On Android, use the native share sheet through the platform adapter.
- On the PWA, use `navigator.share` for text and files where supported; otherwise copy the text and
  download the image.
- If sharing is cancelled, do not show an error; return to the outing.
- The recap is generated locally; no server round-trip is required.

## Privacy

- The image contains only adult display names, student display names, amounts, place, and date.
- Receipt photos are not embedded by default.
- The footer reminds the group that OCR runs on-device.
